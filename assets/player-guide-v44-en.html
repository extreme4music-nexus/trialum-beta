1. Starting your world

Trialum is an offline-first, long-term fictional evolution and life simulation. You start with elements, energy, biomass, insight and a laboratory action allowance, but no pets, habitats or fully grown plants. The game has no final victory screen.

  1. Create a new world or optionally connect Google Play Games and restore a compatible cloud save.
  2. Open Lab and synthesize molecules; gather elements and scientific insight.
  3. Read the next evolutionary-stage requirements before spending resources.
  4. Make periodic backups. Ordinary JSON does not include private conversation memories; use encrypted migration archives to move private data.

2. Main screen and interactive HUD

Five main destinations are Lab, Habitat, Pets, Research and More. The top HUD displays Day, XP, Credits, Molecules, Biomass and Insight. Each indicator is a button with current information and a helpful destination.

  1. Tap Day to inspect and purchase simulated day-skip passes, then redeem them only when ready.
  2. Tap XP for pet growth; Credits for spending and earning; Molecules, Biomass or Insight for current requirements.
  3. Use the bottom navigation for major tasks and More for Food, Flora, Friends, Messages, shared playground, settings and backups.
  4. Select a pet in the living world to inspect its individual needs and history.

3. Laboratory — creating life

The Laboratory is the starting point for new molecules, scientific analysis, geological elements and cellular growth. A basic synthesis produces eight molecular units and two insight, using one lab action and required elements. Analysis adds seven insight and costs one lab action and five energy. Geological expedition uses a lab action.

  1. Synthesize molecules whenever you have the required elements and an available lab action.
  2. Use Scientific Analysis when insight is insufficient for your next research or evolution goal.
  3. Collect geological elements when chemistry stocks run low.
  4. If lab actions are exhausted, use the in-context HUD to buy actions at one earned credit each, subject to the action cap.

4. The eight evolutionary stages

Trialum develops from Atomic Ocean through Organic Molecules, Protocell, Self-Sustaining Cell, Colony, Tissues and Organelles, New Creatures and Living Ecosystem. Evolution requires specific insight, molecules, cell numbers and elapsed time; research continues beyond the eight base stages. Early real-time gates are independent of paid skipped game days.

  1. Open Lab and inspect the next phase, its insight, molecular and cell requirements.
  2. Complete the missing requirements; the first protocell and self-sustaining cells also require initial real elapsed minutes.
  3. Activate Evolution only when every required condition is met.
  4. Advancing a paid in-game day is not a shortcut around other laboratory or real-time research gates.

5. Building your first habitat

Habitat construction starts once your world reaches the required stage. Original environments include Turquoise Lagoon, Crystal Cave and Bioluminescent Garden, each with its own capacity. Construction consists of five resource-dependent steps rather than one instant action.

  1. Reach the required evolutionary stage and open Habitat.
  2. Select a biome and check the construction materials and available capacity.
  3. Complete framework, protective shell, biochemical solution, macro-/micro-elements and flora establishment.
  4. Select the finished habitat to start bringing creatures and compatible plants.

6. Habitat management and biochemistry

Each habitat has separate pH, temperature, oxygen, cleanliness, toxins, mineral levels, plant space and animal capacity. Preferred approximate reference points include pH 7.1 and 24°C. Poor quality reduces creature well-being. Maintenance actions replenish macro-/micro-elements, oxygen, pH/temperature balance and cleanliness.

  1. Open Habitat and inspect the active habitat's quality and chemistry.
  2. Check oxygen, toxins, cleanliness, macro-/micro-elements and plant load before they become critical.
  3. Use maintenance actions when needed; each consumes habitat actions and sometimes elements.
  4. If habitat actions are exhausted, refill the correct habitat directly through the action HUD.

7. Your individual pets

Each pet has a unique identity, age, satiety, joy, energy, cleanliness, bond, skills and its own action allowance. A pet's memory exists independently of any LLM. Caring, feeding, playing, washing, resting and training affect its growth and needs.

  1. Select a pet in the world or Pets list.
  2. Inspect satiety, joy, energy and cleanliness, then choose appropriate care activities.
  3. Remember that food must be placed in the pet's own home reserve before prepared feeding.
  4. When this specific pet uses all its actions, its contextual top-up is separate from other pets; one credit adds one action.

8. Pet evolution and training

Pet evolution is independent of the eight world phases. Training unlocks gradually through pet levels and laboratory progression, with skills such as Wave, Spin, Leap, Symbols, Logic and Dialogue. Performing a previously learned trick does not re-purchase its training.

  1. Select the intended pet and inspect its personal level and laboratory requirements.
  2. Open Pets > Training and choose an unlocked skill.
  3. Training uses a pet action and can earn a game credit and XP.
  4. Higher development keeps the same pet identity, lineage and lifelong memories through compatible DNA transformations.

9. Scientific research and the laboratory institute

Research disciplines cover Molecular Systems, Ecology, Nutrition, Cognition and Fictional Genetics. Each field contributes to laboratory upgrades, foods, materials and future intelligent life. Higher institutes may require pet levels, habitats, environmental quality, time and materials.

  1. Choose Research and inspect the currently unlocked disciplines.
  2. Study a field when sufficient laboratory actions, molecules and energy are available.
  3. Check the detailed next institute requirements and missing values.
  4. Improve earlier prerequisites instead of trying to spend credits to bypass research locks.

10. Advanced species and DNA transformations

Original species include Lumira, Kristel, Nubela and Florix. Later lines Synapso, Teralin, Orbivox, Noetrium, Astralis, Mnemos and Etheron develop by transforming compatible mature existing pets, not creating a new advanced creature from nothing. The same pet keeps its memories and identity.

  1. Open DNA after the correct laboratory level and creature evolution unlock it.
  2. Choose a mature compatible source pet and inspect the next form's research, level, habitat and day requirements.
  3. Meet the prerequisites, then perform the transformation.
  4. Use appearance customization separately from biological progression and keep backups before experiments.

11. Flora and the complete plant lifecycle

Plants are living organisms with growth, health, hydration and compatible aquatic/terrestrial media. Discover a plant model, produce seedlings in the lab, cultivate in beds or an aquatic greenhouse pool, care for it, and transplant mature healthy specimens to suitable habitats. Neglect can cause death.

  1. Open More > Flora, research and unlock the correct botanical model.
  2. Produce seedlings using laboratory actions and raw elements.
  3. Plant seedlings in an appropriate greenhouse bed or pool; monitor hydration and health and use additives as needed.
  4. When an eligible plant reaches the required growth and health, transplant it to a habitat with compatible environment and space.
  5. Before spending day-skip passes, water vulnerable plants — a skipped day runs dehydration and potential plant death.

12. Food production, warehouse and personal reserves

Food is not teleported from a recipe to the pet. The complete route is Nutrition Laboratory → Food Warehouse → Pet Home Reserve → Feed. The laboratory creates portions, the warehouse tracks shelves/cold storage/packing, and each creature owns a private home food reserve.

  1. Open More > Food and produce a compatible recipe; use available laboratory actions, molecules and biomass.
  2. Open Warehouse; locate portions in shelves, cold storage or packing.
  3. Open Pets, select the exact pet, and transfer portions to its Personal Food at Home reserve.
  4. Use Feed when that pet has compatible food and a personal action. Production alone does not feed an animal.

13. More — additional systems and settings

More contains DNA, Flora, Food, Warehouse, Friends, Messages, local and shared playgrounds, Google Play Games, Gallery, Journal, Notifications, Settings and About & Help. Some functions are optional, and locked functions must explain their requirements.

  1. Use More to reach features not in the five main navigation buttons.
  2. Use Journal to inspect major events, pet-care actions, discoveries and progression.
  3. Use Settings for language, theme, audio, notifications, backups and the legacy Free Credits panel.
  4. Use About & Help or the contextual Guide overlay for current offline help.

14. Pet chat, memory and optional local AI

Every pet has lifelong model-independent local memory. Basic English private guidance works without an LLM; evolved pets may optionally use compatible downloaded local Mini/Advanced models after unlock and consent. In 1.4 v44 beta, private pet communication is English-only, even when the game interface uses another language.

  1. Open Pets, select any pet, and use the private chat for gameplay questions such as 'Where do I get food?' or 'What should I do next?'.
  2. Without local AI, the scripted assistant answers known guide/game-state questions; it does not pretend to invent model responses.
  3. Where eligible, enable AI with consent, choose a local model and download it manually; changing models does not erase memories.
  4. Android microphone input and non-network TTS are optional and require compatible installed speech services. Private chat data must not be sent to shared rooms.

15. Friends, messages and shared social features

Friends, invites, messaging and shared rooms are optional online services. Social functions require a voluntarily accepted social agreement and an eligible Google-shared 18+ age band. Unknown, refused, child-managed and under-18 signals remain offline, even in beta. The server must independently enforce authorization.

  1. Open More > Friends and inspect your local eligibility status.
  2. If eligible, explicitly request the Android Google Play Age Signal check and consent to the separate social terms.
  3. Only after authorization create your profile and accept or send friendship invitations.
  4. Avoid personal names, addresses, phone numbers and private owner conversations in public names, messages and shared-room AI speech.

16. Earned credits, extra actions and achievements

Credits can be earned in play through pet training, care, research, discoveries, evolution and achievements according to the actual unlocked activities. In v44 one depleted action can be restored for one earned credit via a contextual HUD. The legacy Free Credits Settings panel remains available; its older fixed prices are separate. Real-money purchases are disabled in beta.

  1. When you run out of actions, use the inline Restore Actions control in Lab, Pet, Habitat or Greenhouse.
  2. Buy exactly the number of actions you need at 1 earned credit per action, within the relevant action limit.
  3. To earn more credits, train and care for pets, conduct available research, unlock development milestones or earn achievements.
  4. Tap Credits in the top HUD to see your current total, history and what it is useful for.

17. Saving and protecting the living world

Trialum saves the world locally. A standard portable JSON export excludes owner-private AI conversations and lifelong private memories. Use the separately encrypted migration archive to preserve the full world, private cognitive memories and locally saved messages. Keep the recovery code separately and off-device; without it, restoration is impossible.

  1. Open More > Settings > Backup & Restore.
  2. For everyday portable world state export JSON; note it does not include private histories.
  3. For moving to another device create an encrypted migration archive and safely store its one-time recovery code.
  4. Before restoring or uninstalling, preserve an off-device copy; restoration replaces local data only after confirmation.

18. Recommended daily routine and what to do next

Trialum is a long-term world and you do not need to play continuously. A sensible sequence is: inspect next evolution/resource requirements, work in the Lab, check habitats, care for pets, water plants, supply food reserves and verify the save. Contextual Help uses your actual current world state to recommend the most useful next step.

  1. Check the top HUD and next-stage goals.
  2. Spend laboratory/research actions where they unlock missing resources.
  3. Inspect habitat chemistry, pet satiety and actions, plant hydration and growth.
  4. Produce food, move it from Warehouse into each pet's home reserve and then feed.
  5. Review Journal and backup status. Never spend credits blindly when the problem is a locked feature, not missing actions.

19. Practical tips and common mistakes

Do not confuse food production with distribution, day passes with safe time travel, model availability with memory, or social beta access with a verified adult age signal. Evolution gates still require their own resources. Each pet has separate actions, and each habitat has distinct chemical conditions.

  1. Before unlocking a new stage, confirm all insight, molecule, cell, research and time requirements.
  2. Check home food reserves if a pet cannot be fed; check species compatibility and personal actions too.
  3. Monitor hydration before using day-skip coupons, because plants can die in the real simulation.
  4. Keep the recovery code for encrypted backups, and avoid private data in shared-room conversations.

20. One world, endless evolution

Trialum is designed to evolve with its player, not finish in an afternoon. From lifeless structures to cells, habitats, varied species, independent creature memories, simulated cultures and long-term ecosystems, your world accumulates a history of real gameplay choices. Every pet is an individual, every habitat is part of the world and every discovery adds to its story.

  1. Play at your own pace.
  2. Experiment with compatible plants, pet lineages, learning and habitats.
  3. Respect resource limits, privacy and optional online features.
  4. Keep your world and its private memories safe with periodic backups.
