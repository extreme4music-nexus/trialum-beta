Privacy Policy

Trialum · Living World edition

Effective date: 18 November 2026

This notice describes the Trialum Android app package com.trialum.ziivaiskra and the optional services shipped with this beta. It explains what stays on your device and what is sent when you choose online or cloud features.

1. Who operates Trialum and how to contact us

Trialum is an independently developed game. For privacy questions, data-access or deletion requests, contact the developer at toniadrenalin@gmail.com or +389 78 371 741. Do not include passwords, one-time codes, social-profile credentials, or private signing information in a support request.

2. You can play offline

The core game is designed to work offline. Your world, pets, progress, journal and other game state are stored locally on your device. Trialum does not require a Trialum password, email address, phone number, contacts list, or real-name account to play the offline game.

Local AI replies are generated on your device after you choose to download and use a language model. The model download is requested from its hosting service; that service may receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address and download request. Your private prompt and locally generated reply are not sent to Trialum as part of local inference.

3. Optional Trialum social features

If you choose to create a social profile or use Friends, messages, visits, shared rooms, presence, or room-based pet conversations, the app sends information over HTTPS or WSS to the Trialum social server. The social profile is separate from Google sign-in. The server does not receive your Google password or Google account email from this feature.

  • Social profile: an in-game nickname, a pseudonymous profile/credential identifier, and the identifiers needed to connect your profile to friends and your pets.
  • Friend features: invitation codes and their expiry, friendship/block relationships, selected pet/profile details, room membership, visits and temporary presence/session identifiers.
  • Messages and reports: preset or short text messages when available, delivery/read/deletion actions, mute settings, and a report category if you choose to report a message. Messages are available only within friend relationships; there is no public chat. Message text is not used for advertising or ranking.
  • Shared pet dialogue: if you opt in to a shared room conversation, short fictional pet dialogue lines and room, pet, turn and session identifiers are sent to the server so they can be relayed to authorized room participants. The player's private conversation with the on-device model is not relayed.
  • Connection information: the server and network providers may process IP address, request time and technical connection data when your device connects. The app itself does not ask for your real-world location.

You can delete your Trialum social profile from the in-game social settings. This deletes the profile and linked social records held by the Trialum service. Invitations expire after 15 minutes; message and notification cards after up to 7 days; temporary presence after up to 2 hours; and message reports after up to 30 days. Some records may remain where needed to address abuse, resolve security incidents, or meet legal obligations.

4. Notifications (optional)

Push notifications are off until you enable them in the game and grant Android notification permission where required. If enabled, Firebase Cloud Messaging creates a delivery token/installation identifier. Trialum sends the token to its social server, linked to your social profile, so the server can request a generic notification for a message. Notification text does not include the message body. Turning push off disables registration and requests removal of the token from the Trialum service. Google may process Firebase installation and technical data under its own terms and privacy information.

5. Google Play Games and cloud saves (optional)

If you connect Google Play Games, Google handles Google account authentication. Trialum receives the Play Games player identifier and public player name needed to show your profile and use Play Games features. The app may send achievement progress and leaderboard scores to Google Play Games Services.

Cloud Save is separately opt-in. If enabled, Trialum sends your game-world save snapshot to Google Play Games Saved Games so you can check, save, or restore it. A snapshot can contain your game progress and the names or other content you entered in your local world. Google processes this data under Google's applicable terms and privacy policy. You can continue to play offline without connecting Google Play Games.

Play Age Signals: The app may request an age-related signal from Google Play to decide whether optional social features can be enabled. If the player or parent/guardian chooses to share it, the app processes the returned age range and related sharing/status on that device for this eligibility check. The age signal is not sent to the Trialum social server and is not used for advertising, analytics, or profiling. If it is unavailable or not shared, social features remain locked while offline play remains available. Google Play handles the request under Google's terms and privacy information. This check is not a substitute for parental consent or supervision where required.

6. Nearby connections and sharing

If you start nearby friend discovery or direct sharing, Android and Google Play services may use Bluetooth or nearby-device APIs to find and connect devices. Older Android versions may request a location-related permission because of that operating system's Bluetooth scanning rules; Trialum does not use this permission to determine or store your physical location. Data you choose to share is sent to the selected nearby participant. You may also choose Android's share sheet to send an invitation or exported file through another app; that app's own privacy terms then apply.

7. Diagnostics you export

Trialum does not automatically upload crash reports. A limited diagnostic record is stored on your device to help troubleshoot app and Play Games errors. If you tap the export control, the JSON report can include the app version and package, signing-certificate SHA-1, device manufacturer/model, Android API level, timestamps, recent app/Play Games diagnostic events and an uncaught-exception stack trace. The report is shared only through the Android share flow you choose. Review it before sending; the receiving app and recipient will process it under their own policies.

8. Data retained locally, deletion and backups

Local game data remains on your device until you delete it, clear app storage, or uninstall the app (Android backup behavior may depend on device settings). JSON export files are saved or shared only when you request them. Google Play Games cloud snapshots are controlled by your opt-in and Google account; removing local data does not necessarily delete a Google-hosted snapshot. Use Google's controls or contact us if you need help with a cloud save.

How to request deletion: In Trialum, open More → Friends → Delete social profile and confirm. To request help outside the app, email toniadrenalin@gmail.com with “Trialum account and data deletion” in the subject. Include only the in-game nickname or profile ID needed to find the account; never send a password or secret credential. Deletion removes the social profile, friend links, and linked room/profile data. Message records are encrypted at rest on the Trialum server, are not end-to-end encrypted, and are deleted when they expire (within 7 days of sending) or earlier when both participants delete them. Invitations and other temporary records expire under their stated periods. A message report may be retained for up to 30 days for safety review; the deleted profile identifier is removed. This does not delete locally stored game saves or Google Play Games cloud saves; use the device and Google account controls for those.

9. Security and service providers

Online connections from the app to Trialum use HTTPS/WSS. When social messaging is used, message text is sent to and processed by the Trialum social server for delivery. The server encrypts message text at rest in its database using a server-managed key. This is not end-to-end encryption: the server processes plaintext and authorized service operators could access it. Message records expire within 7 days and are automatically deleted after expiry, or earlier when both participants delete them. Message reports may be retained for up to 30 days for safety review, with the deleted profile identifier removed. Push registration tokens are encrypted in the server database. Google Play Games and Firebase are provided by Google; optional model files are hosted by the model provider. These providers process data needed for their services under their own terms. No network service can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. Ads, analytics and payments in this beta

Beta 1.0 contains no advertising or advertising SDK, no in-app purchase/billing flow, and no analytics or crash-reporting SDK. Credits and other game resources are earned and used only inside the game in this version; they are not cryptocurrency, cannot be exchanged for cash, and cannot be purchased in this beta. Future features will require an updated privacy notice before they are enabled.

11. Children and families

Trialum has offline play, and its optional online features involve nicknames, friend interactions and messages. A parent or guardian should help a child decide whether to use online social, messaging, cloud-save or nearby features. Do not include real names, contact details, school information, location, or other sensitive information in a nickname or message. Parents or guardians may contact the developer to ask about or request deletion of a child's social data.

12. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the app's data practices materially change. The effective date at the top shows the latest revision. Changes to future game versions, including purchases or new online features, will be disclosed before those features are introduced.
